Neon Savings Check: Help and Support

Last updated October 20, 2026. App published by Neon.

Contact us

Email neon.official@tuta.com. We read every message and usually answer within two days. Please do not send photos of receipts that show your card number.

How do I check a receipt?

On the Check tab tap Scan a receipt, or Choose photos to pick several at once. Lay the receipt flat in good light and fill the screen with it. You get one answer at the top: "All good", or how many things to check and how much money may be at stake.

What does the app check?

That the math adds up, that the deals you checked off came off (including BOGO, "2 for $5", "buy 3" and digital-coupon deals), items that rang up twice, weighed items (weight × price per lb), the sales tax for your state, and, if you snapped shelf tags, that items rang up at the tag price. It can miss things and is not a guarantee: always compare with the paper receipt.

What is "Take it to the desk"?

When something looks overcharged, the app makes a short card for the service desk: each item with the price you expected and the price charged, the total, the receipt date, time and transaction number, a photo of each line, and a polite script. Share it as a picture or PDF; card and account numbers are never shown. It also shows general information about your state's pricing rules, which is not legal advice.

How do I use the deals checklist?

On the Deals tab, add the deals from the weekly ad and check off the ones you are buying. After you scan the receipt, the Deals tab shows which came off, which are missing, and which were not found.

My receipt is too long for one photo.

Tap "Long receipt? Take it in parts" on the Check tab and take photos from the top down, with a line or two of overlap. The app joins them and counts the overlap once. You can also add a part later from the receipt's details.

Can I check an e-receipt?

Yes. Tap "Open an e-receipt (PDF)" on the Check tab, or open the PDF in Mail or Files, tap Share and choose Neon Savings Check. A screenshot of an e-receipt works too: choose it like a photo.

Why does it ask for my state?

Only to check the sales tax on your receipts (some states tax groceries, others do not). It is saved on your phone and you can change it in More. With "Show advanced tools" on you can also type your local tax rate.

Why does a receipt say "things to check"?

Each item in "What to check" says why. Fix any misread line from "Show all the details", or tap the button under it ("This one is fine", "That's right, I bought them", or "I checked it, it's right"). Receipts whose numbers do not add up are not counted in your savings totals until you check them.

What is "Money caught"?

Money you asked the store for after a price check. Mark it Asked or Refunded on the desk card. It is shown on the Savings tab, apart from your coupon and deal savings.

The "You saved" number is wrong.

Open the receipt, tap Show all the details, and type the number from the paper receipt in "The receipt's You saved number is". That number is used from then on.

The date is wrong or missing.

Open the receipt and pick the date of the trip. Receipts with no date, a date in the future, or a date more than 3 months ago are not counted until you check the date.

The camera does not open.

The camera permission is turned off. Tap Open Settings on the Check tab (or open your phone Settings, find Neon Savings Check) and turn on Camera. You can always choose photos you already took instead.

How do I move to a new phone?

In More tap Make a backup file, choose With photos, and save it to your email, Files, iCloud Drive or Google Drive. On the new phone install the app, then More > Restore from a backup file.

Which stores work?

The app was built and tested with real US grocery receipts. Receipts from some stores may need more fixing by hand. The app is not made by, affiliated with, or endorsed by any store.
