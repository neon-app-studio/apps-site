Scroll to Stove: Terms, Privacy and Copyright

Last updated October 30, 2026. App published by Neon.

The short version

Scroll to Stove is a private recipe box for your own use. There is no account, no ads and no tracking.

Your recipes stay on this iPhone. We (the app maker) never receive them.

What stays on your iPhone

Recipes you save, your grocery list and your settings are stored only on this iPhone, inside the app. Deleting the app erases them. A backup file is made only when you tap Save a backup, and it goes only where you choose.

Screenshots and camera photos you pick are read on this iPhone to find the recipe text. The pictures themselves are not kept or uploaded.

If Apple Intelligence is on, it runs on this iPhone to tidy messy captions. Nothing is sent to us.

Adding from a link

When you paste a link, the app makes one request for that one post or page to read its written caption or recipe text, and identifies itself as Scroll to Stove. For TikTok links it uses TikTok's public oEmbed service.

The app never downloads, saves or re-posts videos, photos or audio from any site, never crawls other pages, and never reads anything you did not choose.

Credit to creators

Every recipe keeps the creator's name and a link back to the original post when they are known, and shows them at the top of the recipe. Sharing a recipe always starts with the creator and the link, and you can share just the link.

Creators keep all rights to their recipes, captions, photos and videos. Saving a recipe here does not make it yours to publish.

Your responsibility

Use the app for your own personal, non-commercial cooking. You are responsible for what you save and share, and for following the terms of the sites and apps you save from.

Recipes are read automatically and can contain mistakes. Check amounts, temperatures, allergens and food safety yourself.

Creators: copyright and takedown

If you are a creator and want a recipe source blocked from the app, or have any copyright concern, email neon.official@tuta.com with "COPYRIGHT" in the subject, a link to your original post, and how to reach you. We reply within 3 business days. Because saved recipes live only on each person's iPhone, we cannot see or delete them, but we can block a source from link import in an update.

Sharing and selling data

We do not collect, sell or share any personal data. When you share a recipe, the text goes only where you send it.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we collect no personal information from anyone.

Not affiliated

Scroll to Stove is an independent app. It is not made by, affiliated with or endorsed by any social media app, website or creator. Their names belong to their owners.
