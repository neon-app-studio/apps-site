How do I add a recipe?

Tap +. Pick screenshots of the recipe, take a photo of a recipe card or cookbook page, paste a caption or a link, or type it in. Check the recipe, then tap Save.

A link did not work.

Some posts only have the recipe in the video, or the site does not let the app read the page. Copy the caption and paste it, or take screenshots of the recipe and add those.

How do I move my recipes to a new phone?

In Settings tap Save a backup and save the file to Files or iCloud Drive. On the new phone install the app, then Settings > Restore from a backup.

I bought the unlock. How do I get it back?

In Settings tap Restore Purchase while signed in with the same Apple Account. If that does not work, tap Unlock: Apple will tell you that you already own it and will not charge you again.
