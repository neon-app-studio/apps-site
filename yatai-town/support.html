Yatai Town: Help and Support

Last updated November 1, 2026.

Yatai Town is a cozy match-3 puzzle game at a little food stall under paper lanterns. Tap pieces, match three of a kind, and collect the goal items before the timer runs out.

Contact us

Email neon.official@tuta.com. We read every message and usually answer within two days. If a level seems impossible or something looks wrong, tell us the level number.

How do I play?

Tap a piece in the pile to move it to the tray. When three of the same kind are in the tray, they clear. The goal items are shown at the top of the screen. Collect them all before the timer runs out. The tray has seven spaces, so if it fills up the level is lost.

What do the tools do?

Ladle: stirs the pile so new pieces come to the top. Chopsticks: pull a set of 3 out of the pile for you. Spatula: flips your last pick back onto the pile. Sand timer: stops the clock for 15 seconds. You earn tools as rewards and can also trade coins for them.

What are the glowing prizes in the pile?

Most levels hide one or two table prizes in the pile, like a lucky coin purse, a festival bundle or a lacquer treasure box. Tap one to collect it for bonus coins or a free tool. Prizes never use a tray space and you don't need them to win. Some are buried under other pieces, so keep an eye out as the pile goes down. Each prize can be collected once.

How do lives work?

You have up to 5 lives. Losing a level costs one. Lives come back for free, one every 30 minutes, even while the app is closed. Win 5 levels in a row and you get 15 minutes of unlimited lives.

What are unlimited lives?

When you run out of lives, you can wait for a free one or buy unlimited lives for 15 minutes or 1 hour. While it is on, losing a level costs no lives and a countdown shows on screen. It runs on real time, so it keeps counting while the app is closed. Time you buy while unlimited is already on is added to the time left.

I paid but did not get my time.

Close the app completely and open it again. A purchase that did not finish is handed to the game again on the next launch. If it still does not show, email us. For refunds, use Apple's Report a Problem page.

I hear no sound.

Turn up your iPhone volume. The home and pause screens have separate Music and Sound buttons, so check that both are on.

Is my progress saved?

Yes. Your level, coins, tools, stall upgrades and food stamps are saved on your phone. Deleting the app deletes them.

Are there ads?

No. There are no ads and no account, and the game works without an internet connection. The only purchases are the optional unlimited lives.
