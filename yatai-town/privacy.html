Yatai Town: Privacy Policy

Last updated November 1, 2026.

The short version

Yatai Town does not collect any data. There is no account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What is stored, and where

Your level, lives, coins, tools, stall upgrades, food stamps, table prizes found and the Music and Sound settings are saved only on your device, inside the app. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

In-app purchases

If you buy unlimited lives, Apple handles the payment. We never see your name, Apple Account or payment details. The app only learns that a purchase went through so it can add the time.

Network use

The game, its sounds and its music are all inside the app, so it works without an internet connection. The app does not send anything over the internet, apart from purchases, which go through Apple.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. If the app ever starts collecting data, this page and the App Store privacy label will say so first.

Contact

Questions about privacy: neon.official@tuta.com.
