Evenpage: Help and Support

Last updated November 1, 2026. App published by Neon.

Contact us

Email neon.official@tuta.com. We usually answer within two days.

How does it work?

Each date has one page that holds five years. Tap Write my entry and write a few lines in your own words, then pick a mood and the weather. Next year you will see them on the same page, right above the new ones. Turn your iPhone sideways to see two days side by side.

How do I change the look of a page?

On the book page tap the palette button. Pick a theme for the whole journal, for a month or for a single day. A day theme wins over a month theme, and a month theme wins over the journal theme.

Can it remind me in the evening?

Yes, if you want. In Settings > Reminder tap Set up reminder, then pick a time (8:00 PM to start). The note only asks for a few lines about today, never shows what is in your journal. If it never shows up, turn on Notifications for Evenpage in iPhone Settings.

What is free?

Writing, reading, search, the lock and export are always free, with no limit on how much you write, and so is a starter set of themes. The Full Version is one payment, no subscription. It adds all 12 art themes, every color, gradient and pastel, and themes for a single day or month.

I bought the Full Version. How do I get it back?

In Settings > Full Version tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Ask Apple for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I keep a copy or move to a new phone?

Your entries are included in your normal iPhone or iCloud backup, so restoring a backup to a new phone brings them along. Settings > Export also makes a text copy you can save anywhere.

How do I delete my data?

There is no account and nothing is stored on our side. Delete an entry from its page, use Settings > Delete everything, or delete the app.
