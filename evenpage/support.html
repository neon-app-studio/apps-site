Evenpage: Help and Support

Last updated October 30, 2026. App published by Neon.

Contact us

Email neon.official@tuta.com. We usually answer within two days.

How does a draft work?

In the evening open the app, pick a mood, add a few words if you like, and tap Draft my day. The app uses the photos you took, the places you stopped and your steps to write a short first draft. Edit anything, or tap Write it myself instead.

My draft is empty or says very little.

Drafts need at least one source. In Settings, check that Photos, Steps or Places I stop is on. If iOS shows a source as off, tap Open Settings and allow it there. Places I stop only records days after you turn it on.

What is free?

Writing, reading, search, the lock and export are always free. Your first 7 drafted days are free. The Full Version is one payment for drafts on every day. There is no subscription.

I bought the Full Version. How do I get it back?

In Settings > Full Version tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Ask Apple for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I keep a copy or move to a new phone?

Your entries are included in your normal iPhone or iCloud backup, so restoring a backup to a new phone brings them along. Settings > Export also makes a text copy you can save anywhere.

How do I delete my data?

There is no account and nothing is stored on our side. Delete an entry from its page, use Settings > Delete everything, or delete the app.
