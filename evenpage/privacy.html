Evenpage: Privacy Policy and Terms

Last updated November 1, 2026. App published by Neon.

The short version

Evenpage is a private five-year journal. There is no account, no server, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your journal stays on your iPhone. We (the app maker) never receive it.

Your writing

Every entry is written by you. The app does not write or change your text, and nothing you write is read by a server or an AI or sent anywhere.

What the app uses, and only if you choose to

Photos. The app only sees the photos you pick in Apple's photo picker. A resized copy of each one is saved inside the app with its entry, so it stays even if you delete the original from your library. The app does not read the rest of your photo library, and your photos are never uploaded.

Face ID or passcode. If you turn on the lock, iOS checks your face or passcode. The app only learns whether the check passed.

Notifications (evening reminder). Off unless you set it up. iOS shows one note a day at the time you pick, scheduled on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to us or to any server, and the note never shows anything from your journal.

Each one is optional. The app does not use your location, your steps or other motion data.

Network use

The only thing the app connects to is Apple's App Store, to buy or restore the optional Full Version. Nothing from your journal is sent with it.

Storage, backups and deleting

Entries, photo copies and settings are stored only inside the app on your iPhone. They are included in your normal iPhone or iCloud backup, which Apple handles. Export in Settings makes a text copy that goes only where you choose.

Delete one entry from its page, use Settings > Delete everything, or delete the app to erase it all. Deleting the app erases your journal, so export first if you want to keep it. There is no account, so there is nothing to delete on our side.

Purchases

The app is free to download. The optional one-time Full Version is bought through Apple. Apple handles the payment under its own privacy policy; we never see your card or Apple Account details. Refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Sharing and selling data

We do not collect, sell or share any personal data. When you export or share an entry, it goes only where you send it.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we collect no personal information from anyone.

Terms

Use the app for your own personal journal. Your entries are yours. The app is provided as is; keep your own copies of anything important with Export.
