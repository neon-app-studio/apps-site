Evenpage: Privacy Policy and Terms

Last updated October 30, 2026. App published by Neon.

The short version

Evenpage is a private diary. There is no account, no server, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your diary stays on your iPhone. We (the app maker) never receive it.

What the app reads, and only if you allow it

Photos. The time and location of photos you took on a day, to draft that day's entry. Small copies of the few photos that go in an entry are kept inside the app. Your photo library is never uploaded.

Motion and fitness. The step count and walking distance your iPhone already keeps.

Location (Places I stop). Off unless you turn it on. iOS tells the app when you arrive at and leave a place, so the draft can say where you went. The app keeps the last 45 days of these stops, on your iPhone only.

Face ID or passcode. If you turn on the lock, iOS checks your face or passcode. The app only learns whether the check passed.

Notifications (evening reminder). Off unless you set it up. iOS shows one note a day at the time you pick, scheduled on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to us or to any server, and the note never shows anything from your diary.

Each one is optional. Without any of them you can still write entries yourself.

The one thing that leaves your iPhone

If "Look up place names" is on, a location is sent to Apple's map service to get a name like "the park". Apple handles that under its own privacy policy. It is not sent to us. You can turn it off in Settings.

How drafts are written

On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, the draft is written by Apple's on-device AI model, on the phone. On other iPhones a simple built-in writer is used. Either way nothing about your day is sent to us or to any AI service. Drafts are a starting point and can be wrong: read and edit them before you keep them.

Polish

Polish is optional. It rewrites text you wrote yourself, using only Apple's on-device AI model, entirely on your iPhone. Your words are never sent to us, to Apple or to any AI service, and no data leaves the phone. Before anything changes you see the polished version next to yours and choose. Your own text is always kept with the entry, so you can undo a polish at any time. On iPhones without Apple Intelligence, Polish is simply not available and nothing changes.

Storage, backups and deleting

Entries, photo copies, named places and settings are stored only inside the app on your iPhone. They are included in your normal iPhone or iCloud backup, which Apple handles. Export in Settings makes a text copy that goes only where you choose.

Delete one entry from its page, use Settings > Delete everything, or delete the app to erase it all. Deleting the app erases your diary, so export first if you want to keep it. There is no account, so there is nothing to delete on our side.

Purchases

The app is free to download. The optional one-time Full Version is bought through Apple. Apple handles the payment under its own privacy policy; we never see your card or Apple Account details. Refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Sharing and selling data

We do not collect, sell or share any personal data. When you export or share an entry, it goes only where you send it.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we collect no personal information from anyone.

Terms

Use the app for your own personal diary. Your entries are yours. The app is provided as is; keep your own copies of anything important with Export.
