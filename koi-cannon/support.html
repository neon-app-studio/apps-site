Koi Cannon: Help and Support

Last updated October 29, 2026.

Koi Cannon is a physics game. A koi in a pot of water lobs glowing water orbs at towers of jars, pots and boxes on a night festival street. Knock them all down before you run out of orbs.

Contact us

Email neon.official@tuta.com. We read every message and usually answer within two days. If a level seems impossible or something looks wrong, tell us the level number.

How do I play?

Tap any jar, pot or box and the koi aims and fires at it. You can also aim by hand: pull back on the screen and let go. Each tower stands on a glowing base. Hit the base and the tower comes down. Grey stone never breaks, so knock pieces off it instead.

What does the firecracker do?

From Level 6 a firecracker rolls around the street. Hit it and it explodes, throwing nearby pieces around. Pieces broken by the blast score extra, up to five times the points. It keeps moving, so aim where it is going, not where it is.

Some towers spin. How do I hit them?

From Level 9 some towers stand on turning drums or carousels. Watch the turn and tap a little ahead of the piece you want. Some of them pause or change direction, and that is a good moment to shoot.

How do stars and levels work?

Clear a level to unlock the next one. The fewer orbs you use, the more stars you get, and every orb left over adds bonus points. You can replay any level you have opened from the Levels screen.

I hear no sound.

Turn up your iPhone volume. The ♪ button turns the music on and off. Sound effects stay on either way.

Is my progress saved?

Yes. Your stars and best scores are saved on your phone. Deleting the app deletes them.

Is it free? Are there ads?

Koi Cannon is free. There are no ads, no in-app purchases and no account. It works without an internet connection.

Your data

Koi Cannon collects no data. See the privacy policy.
