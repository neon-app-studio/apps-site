Koi Cannon: Privacy Policy

Last updated October 29, 2026.

The short version

Koi Cannon does not collect any data. There is no account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What is stored, and where

Your stars, best scores and the music on/off setting are saved only on your device, inside the app. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

Network use

The game, its sounds and its music are all inside the app, so it works without an internet connection. The app does not send anything over the internet.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. If the app ever starts collecting data, this page and the App Store privacy label will say so first.

Contact

Questions about privacy: neon.official@tuta.com.
