Koi Cannon: Privacy Policy

Last updated November 22, 2026.

The short version

Koi Cannon does not collect any data. There is no account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What is stored, and where

Your stars, best scores and the music on/off setting are saved only on your device, inside the app. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them. If you buy the storm levels, the app also saves a purchased flag on your phone so it knows they are unlocked.

Purchases

From version 1.1, levels 21 to 100 can be unlocked with one optional purchase. It is sold and handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details or your Apple ID. The app only asks Apple whether the unlock was bought, and remembers the answer on your phone.

Network use

The game, its sounds and its music are all inside the app, so it works without an internet connection. The app does not send anything over the internet. The only exception is the purchase check, which goes through Apple's App Store, not to us.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. If the app ever starts collecting data, this page and the App Store privacy label will say so first.
