Below 400: Privacy Policy

Last updated October 20, 2026. App published by Neon.

The short version

Below 400 does not collect any data. There is no account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your study progress stays on your phone.

What is stored, and where

Your answers, review schedule, practice exam results, starred questions and whether you have unlocked the full version are saved only on your device, inside the app. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them. You can also erase your progress at any time in the app under About, then Reset all progress.

Purchases

The optional one-time full version is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment and your Apple Account; we never see your name, email, card or payment details. The app asks Apple's purchase system whether the full version is owned on your Apple Account so it can unlock it, and that information stays on your device. Apple's handling of purchases is covered by Apple's own privacy policy.

Network use

The app works without an internet connection. The only connection it makes is to Apple's App Store when it checks, buys or restores the full version. If you tap a link to a source document, it opens in your browser, outside the app.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. If the app ever starts collecting data, this page and the App Store privacy label will say so first.
