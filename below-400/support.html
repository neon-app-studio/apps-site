Below 400: Help and Support

Last updated October 20, 2026. App published by Neon Burgess.

Below 400 helps you prepare for the FAA Part 107 Remote Pilot knowledge test (UAG). Not affiliated with or endorsed by the FAA.

Contact us

Email neon.official@tuta.com. We read every message and usually answer within two days. If you think an answer is wrong, tell us the question's first few words; every answer cites its FAA source, and we check it against that source.

What is free?

About a quarter of the questions in every topic, one full practice exam, the figures, the rule-change guide and your readiness score. Explanations and sources are always free on every question you can see.

What does the full version add?

All the questions, unlimited practice exams, weak-spot drills, spaced review and the recurrent refresher. It is a one-time purchase of $11.99 (US). There is no subscription and no ads.

I bought the full version. How do I get it on a new phone?

Install Below 400, sign in to the App Store with the same Apple Account, open the unlock screen and tap Restore Purchases.

The purchase did not unlock the app.

Check your internet connection, then tap Restore Purchases on the unlock screen. If a parent or guardian must approve purchases (Ask to Buy), the app unlocks as soon as the purchase is approved. If it still does not unlock, email us.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all App Store payments and refunds. Request a refund from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How does the practice exam work?

It uses the same format as the knowledge test: 60 questions, 2 hours, 70% to pass, with the same mix of subjects. Your results list the ACS codes to study. A good practice score helps you see where you stand, but the app cannot promise a result on the real test.

How do I reset my progress?

Open About and tap Reset all progress. This erases your answers, review schedule and exam history on this phone and cannot be undone.

Are the charts current?

Chart questions use the FAA testing supplement figures and excerpts from a current FAA sectional chart. They are for study only and must not be used for navigation. Always check current charts and regulations before you fly.

Your data

Below 400 collects no data. Your progress stays on your phone. See the privacy policy.
