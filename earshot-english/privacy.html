Earshot English: Privacy Policy

Last updated October 22, 2026. App published by Neon.

The short version

Earshot English does not collect any data. There is no account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your progress stays on your phone.

What is stored, and where

Your stars, best scores, unlocked levels and settings (such as music, voice volume, Japanese hints, round time and graphics quality) are saved only on your device, inside the app. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

Speech and sound

The English words and sentences are spoken by your iPhone's built-in voice. The app only plays sound; it never uses the microphone and never records you.

Network use

The game, its 3D house, voices and music are all inside the app, so it works without an internet connection. The app does not send anything over the internet.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. If the app ever starts collecting data, this page and the App Store privacy label will say so first.

Contact
