Earshot English: Help and Support

Last updated October 22, 2026. App published by Neon Burgess.

Earshot English is a listening game. You hear an English word or phrase, find that thing somewhere in a 3D house, and tap it.

Contact us

Email neon.official@tuta.com. We read every message and usually answer within two days. If a word sounds wrong or an object is hard to find, tell us the word and the room.

How do I play?

Pick a level and tap Play. Listen to the word, then look around: drag on the screen to look, tap a glowing circle on the floor to walk there, or use the Turn and 180° buttons. When you see the thing you heard, tap it. Tap the speaker button to hear the word again.

What if I can't find a word?

Tap Skip to move on to the next word. Skipping gives no points, but it keeps your streak, and the word comes back later. If the word is in another room, an arrow shows you which way to go, and the Easy help setting points out the answer sooner.

How do the levels unlock?

Earn at least one star on a level to open the next one. The levels go from First words up to Expert words and Phrase master, plus a Bonus level of tricky words for Japanese speakers (katakana traps, L and R, B and V, and similar vowels).

What are the Japanese hints?

Turn on Japanese hints (日本語ヒント) in the menu or the pause screen to see the Japanese meaning under each English word. Leave it off for a pure listening challenge.

Who are the people in the house?

The family: Grandma, Grandpa, Mom, Dad and Ken. Tap any of them to hear a short, clear English sentence about what they are doing. Tapping a person never counts as a wrong answer.

The music or voice is too loud or quiet.

Music and voice have separate volume controls in the menu and the pause screen. You can also turn the music off. If you hear nothing, check that your iPhone is not on silent and the volume is up.

The game feels slow on my phone.

Open the menu and set Graphics to Low or Medium.

Is it free? Are there ads?

Earshot English is free. There are no ads, no in-app purchases and no account.

Your data

Earshot English collects no data. Your progress stays on your phone. See the privacy policy.
